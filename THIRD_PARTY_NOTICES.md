# Third-Party Materials

The source release does not bundle model weights, upstream model examples,
robot assets, or simulator binaries. The generated meshes in the demo are
procedurally generated by this project's source.

Optional components are installed separately and retain their own licenses:

- [MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco): optional scene loader and renderer.
- [Pillow](https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow): optional launch-video image composition.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html): external video encoder; its license depends on the build.
- [NVIDIA Cosmos3 Nano](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano): optional model target;
  consult the model card and license at the specific revision you download.
- Real2Motrix / MotrixSim: external integrations, not distributed here; obtain
  them separately and review their own licensing and compatibility requirements.

The macOS launch-video helper uses an installed system voice and fonts. These
assets are not part of the Apache-2.0 source license and are not redistributed
as standalone files. No upstream sample video or stock music is bundled.
